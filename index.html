How it works & setting the watch

Why a Gregorian year does the trick

The watch works out the weekday from the year, month and day you set. Jalali months have 29–31 days just like Gregorian ones, so if you type the Jalali month and day into the Gregorian date, the watch counts along correctly until the end of the month. The only thing that would be wrong is the weekday — unless you pick a year in which that Gregorian month/day falls on the same weekday as today's Jalali date.

This page searches the watch's calendar range (2000–2099) for such years and picks the one closest to the current year. Every weekday comes around at least once every 28 years, so there is always a match — except for 2-30, 2-31, 4-31 and 6-31, days that simply do not exist in the Gregorian calendar.

Because the two calendars disagree on month lengths, re-check at the start of each Jalali month (the “in sync until” line tells you when).

Setting the date on a 3180 module

  1. In Timekeeping mode, hold A (upper-left, ADJUST) until the city code flashes.
  2. Press C (lower-left, MODE) six times to reach the year.
  3. Use D (lower-right, +) and B (upper-right, −) to dial in the year shown above.
  4. Press C to move to the month, then again for the day, and enter the Jalali month and day.
  5. Press A to exit. The weekday updates by itself.

The same approach works for any digital watch with a 2000–2099 auto-calendar; only the button sequence differs.
